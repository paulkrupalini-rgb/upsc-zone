TODAY'S PRACTICE

Why is fundamental education important for an inclusive society?

Write a short answer covering the introduction, key arguments, and conclusion.

Suggested time: 10 minutes Target: 150 words
0 words

Suggested answer structure

Introduction: Explain how education supports equal opportunity and participation in society.

Key arguments: Discuss awareness of rights, employment opportunities, social mobility, and reduced inequality. Mention barriers such as poverty and unequal access.

Conclusion: Emphasise inclusive, accessible, quality education for all.

Use this as a structure guide, not a model answer to memorise.

REVISION EXERCISE

Urban flooding in Indian cities

Use this practice topic to explore urban planning, drainage, wetland protection, climate resilience, and disaster management.

Think through these questions

  • What natural and human factors contribute to urban flooding?
  • How can city planning reduce flood risk?
  • Which sustainable solutions should local authorities prioritise?
0 words

UPSC issue-analysis framework

1. Causes: Identify the natural, social, economic, and administrative factors.

2. Impacts: Consider effects on people, infrastructure, the economy, and the environment.

3. Challenges: Identify implementation gaps, resource constraints, and competing priorities.

4. Solutions: Suggest practical, sustainable measures and explain how they could be implemented.

5. UPSC connection: Link the issue to relevant syllabus areas, government institutions, or policies.
